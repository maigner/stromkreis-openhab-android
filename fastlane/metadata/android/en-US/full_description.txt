Stromkreis is the open platform by energy communities for energy communities. This app connects members of an Austrian energy community (EEG) to their Stromkreis gateway at home and to the Stromkreis Cloud.

Features:

* Live view of PV system, home battery and grid feed-in
* Forecast-driven battery management: charge the battery when it benefits the community
* Remote access to the gateway from anywhere through the Stromkreis Cloud (https://hac.stromkreis.net)
* Setup in seconds: scan the QR code from your Stromkreis page or open the setup link
* Notifications, NFC tags, voice commands, quick settings tiles and a Tasker plugin
* Native support for phones and tablets

Stromkreis is non-profit and open source. Learn more at https://stromkreis.net

The Stromkreis gateway runs on openHAB; this app is based on the openHAB Android client (Eclipse Public License 2.0).
